\section{Design}

The measurement setup implements the instrumentation required to realize the methods described in the previous section.
The goal is to measure the EMC-relevant high-frequency components of the three main noise contributors: the drain-source voltages of both high-side and low-side switches and the commutation loop current.
In addition, a conventional conducted EMC measurement via two line impedance stabilization networks (LISNs) is included. % for direct comparison with standardized measurement results.

	\centering

The setup is shown as a photograph in \fref{fig:pic_b2b_setup}, and its equivalent circuit is depicted in \fref{fig:ESB_b2b}.
A back-to-back converter topology is employed, in which both half-bridges share the same DC link.
This allows multiple switching events to be generated consecutively at an identical operating point, enabling the operation of a pulse chain and, thereby, increasing the accuracy of the frequency-domain emission analysis.

\begin{figure}[ht]
	\centering
	\import{./figures/6_Environment/}{"Diss_ESB_B2B.pdf_tex"}
	\caption{Equivalent circuit diagram of the back-to-back converter measurement setup.}\label{fig:ESB_b2b}
\end{figure}

The DUTs, \mti{Q}{LS} and \mti{Q}{HS}, are mounted on the primary half-bridge, which is mechanically connected to an aluminum cooler plate.
The cooler does not function as a heat sink here; instead, it establishes a defined, low-impedance current path for the ground currents flowing to the LISNs.
Since the focus here is on the direct measurement of intrinsic EM noise, the interaction between the \mi{v}{DS} measurement and the LISN measurement is not discussed further; a detailed analysis is given in~\cite{kragl_impact_2024}.

% For this paper only the raw emissions are considered and hence, no LISNs measurements are shown.
% For an analysis of the correlation between raw emissions and system-level LISN measurement, please refer to~\cite{kragl_impact_2024}.

The secondary half-bridge drives the complementary switching events and consists of the auxiliary switches \mti{Q}{LS,aux} and \mti{Q}{HS,aux}.
To minimize their contribution to the measured EM emissions, these devices are rated at twice the current of the DUTs and are operated with five times the gate resistance, which reduces their switching speed and the associated high-frequency spectral content.
Furthermore, the auxiliary switches are positioned adjacent to the aluminum ground plate rather than mounted on it, preventing a direct coupling path into the measurement ground.
The DC ports of both half-bridges are high-frequency isolated through the main filters of the two LISNs, ensuring that EM noise generated by the auxiliary half-bridge does not appear in the LISN reference measurements.
All digital signals are transmitted via optical fibers, and the gate-driver power supply is provided by batteries.
