\chapter{Fundamentals for Electromagnetic Emissions of Power Semiconductor Devices}
\label{ch:Fundamentals}

This chapter first establishes how system-level EM emissions relevant to EMC compliance originate in semiconductor devices and propagate through power-electronic systems. 
This discussion motivates the measurement of the raw, intrinsic emissions generated by individual power semiconductor devices.
It then reviews error sources in dynamic power-electronic measurements, providing the background for the verification challenges addressed in Chapters~\ref{ch:voltage} and~\ref{ch:AShunt}.
Finally, the chapter discusses the measurement limitations specific to semiconductor-focused EM emission analysis and reviews how previous work has addressed them.
