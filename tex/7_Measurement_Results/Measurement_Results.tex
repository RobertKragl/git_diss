\chapter{Measurement Results (15-25 pages)}

The measurement results obtained with the setup described in the previous section are presented and analyzed in this section.
The focus is on demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed methods for capturing the high-frequency components of the drain-source voltages and commutation-loop current.
First, the impact of the individual noise-reduction steps is shown for one representative measurement (\mi{v}{DS,LS}).
Afterward, the influence of the active area on the high-frequency components of the switching transient is investigated.
The active area is one of the easiest parameters to vary in the design of a semiconductor device within a given technology, and it has a significant influence on switching behavior and thus on EM noise generation~\cite{stephan_schwaiger_advanced_2023}.
% This is one example; the setup enables the study of a broad range of operating and component parameters.
Lastly, a market overview is given, comparing the EM noise emission performance of different semiconductor manufacturers.
